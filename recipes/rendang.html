Rendang

Delicious Indonesian beef curry in a rich, spicy sauce.

Img Source : Berapa lama masak rendang? https://azko.id/inspiration/berapa-lama-masak-rendang

Ingredients

Recipe by Anggreini Lukastuti https://cookpad.com/id/resep/26417710?ref=search&search_term=rendang+daging

    Main Ingredients

  • 500 g Beef (top side / gandik / outer part of the hind leg)
  • 400 g Mini potatoes / baby potatoes

    Seasoning

  • 1 packet Sajiku Rendang instant seasoning paste
  • 700 ml Total coconut milk (500 ml thin / diluted + 200 ml thick)
  • 1/2 tsp Baking soda (for tenderizing the beef)
  • 1/2 tsp Stock powder
  • 1/2 tsp Granulated sugar

Steps

  1. Tenderize & Rinse: Coat the beef slices with baking soda, wait 10 minutes, then rinse thoroughly.
  2. Mix Seasoning: Place beef in a cold pan, add the instant seasoning packet, and mix until fully coated.
  3. First Simmer (20 mins): Add potatoes and thin coconut milk. Cook over medium heat for 20 minutes.
  4. Final Reduction (20 mins): Stir in thick coconut milk, stock powder, and sugar. Cook on medium heat for another 20 minutes until the sauce thickens and reaches your preferred consistency.
