Mie Martabak

Delicious Indonesian street food, a savory pancake filled with noodles and eggs.

Img Source: Martabak Mie Telur Kornethttps://www.royco.co.id/r/martabak-mie-telur-kornet.html/125715

Ingredients

Recipe by Aulia https://cookpad.com/id/resep/26723429?ref=search&search_term=martabak+mie

  • 1 packet Indomie (or any prefereable fried instant noodle brand)
  • 1 Egg
  • Cooking Oil
  • (Opt) Scallions

Steps

  1. Boil & Drain: Cook the instant noodles in boiling water until soft, then drain completely.
  2. Mix: In a bowl, combine the cooked noodles, egg, instant noodle seasoning, and scallions. Stir until well mixed.
  3. Fry: Heat a small amount of oil in a pan over low heat. Pour in the noodle mixture.
  4. Crisp & Serve: Fry over low heat until fully cooked and golden brown on both sides. Drain excess oil and serve hot.
