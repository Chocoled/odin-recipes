Soda Gembira

Popular Indonesian sweet mocktail made by combining carbonated soda water, red cocopandan syrup, and sweetened condensed milk served over ice

Img Source: Es Soda Gembirahttps://cookpad.com/id/resep/25800773?ref=search&search_term=soda+gembira

Ingredients

Recipe by Ratih Sukardihttps://cookpad.com/id/resep/25800773?ref=search&search_term=soda+gembira

  • 1 sachet (~37 g) Sweetened condensed milk (white)
  • 250 ml soda/carbonated water
  • Ice Cubes

Steps

  1. Layer: Pour the sweetened condensed milk into a glass.
  2. Add Soda: Fill the glass with ice cubes, then pour the red soda over top.
  3. Mix: Stir well until fully combined and creamy. Serve chilled.
